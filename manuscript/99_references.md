# References

The full BibTeX source lives in `manuscript/references.bib`. Key works behind
the mission models:

1. P. C. Mahalanobis, "On the generalized distance in statistics,"
   *Proceedings of the National Institute of Sciences of India*, 2:49-55, 1936
   (multivariate identity screen).
2. V. I. Levenshtein, "Binary codes capable of correcting deletions,
   insertions, and reversals," *Soviet Physics Doklady*, 10(8):707-710, 1966
   (edit-distance sequence scoring).
3. L. W. Gold, "Use of ice covers for transportation," *Canadian Geotechnical
   Journal*, 8(2):170-181, 1971 (ice load-capacity rule).
4. D. S. Sodhi, "Breakthrough loads of floating ice sheets," *Journal of Cold
   Regions Engineering*, 9(1):4-22, 1995 (ice sheet loading).
5. P. V. Hobbs, *Ice Physics*, Clarendon Press, 1974 (ice mechanical and
   thermal properties).
6. M. J. Tipton, "The initial responses to cold-water immersion in man,"
   *Clinical Science*, 77(6):581-588, 1989 (cold-shock response).
7. F. Golden & M. J. Tipton, *Essentials of Sea Survival*, Human Kinetics,
   2002 (immersion survival timeline).
8. I. Newton, "Scala graduum caloris," *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal
   Society*, 22:824-829, 1701 (cooling law).
9. R. H. Battin, *An Introduction to the Mathematics and Methods of
   Astrodynamics*, AIAA, 1999 (orbital propagation).
10. P. E. Glaser, "Power from the sun: its future," *Science*, 162(3856):857-861,
    1968 (orbital reflector / space solar power).
